Wind Power

How it works

Wind turns turbine blades, which spin a generator to produce electricity.

Main Benefits

Renewable, clean, and low operating cost after installation.

Best Locations

Open fields, coastal areas, and high wind zones.

Environmental Impact

No emissions, but can affect birds and landscape views.

Limitations

Depends on wind availability and weather conditions.
