Solar Power

How it works

Solar panels convert sunlight into electricity using photovoltaic cells.

Main Benefits

Clean, renewable, and reduces electricity costs over time.

Best Applications

Homes, schools, and remote areas with strong sunlight.

Environmental Impact

No pollution and helps reduce carbon emissions.

Limitations

Depends on weather and sunlight availability.
