Hydro Power

How it works

Flowing water spins turbines connected to generators that produce electricity.

Main Benefits

Very efficient, reliable, and produces large amounts of energy.

Best Locations

Dams, rivers, and areas with strong water flow.

Environmental Impact

Can affect ecosystems and fish migration patterns.

Limitations

Requires large infrastructure and water availability.
